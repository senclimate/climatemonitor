Data & Methods
Daily Sea Surface Temperature (SST) from NOAA OISST v2.1 (0.25° global grid). Climate mode indices are calculated following Zhao et al. (2024, Nature). Anomalies use the 1991–2020 climatology; detrended indices are linearly detrended over 1982–2025.
